Why Evella

It's yours, or it doesn't work

A meditation about “abundance” is about nobody. Evella only ever says things you actually said, back to you.

🕊️

Your words, not a template

Your name, your people, the sentence you actually spoke. No blanks filled into someone else's script.

🎧

Narrated, not read out

A real voice at a real pace, with birdsong and running water underneath it. Made for headphones.

⏱️

Three minutes

Short enough to finish before you leave the house. That's the whole point — you'll actually do it tomorrow.

🌅

A new one each morning

Not a library you scroll until you give up. One session, built this morning, from what you've told it so far.

📷

See yourself there

Add a photo and Evella shows you the life you just described — with you in it.

🔒

You can see what it knows

Everything Evella remembers about you is listed in plain words, and you can delete any line of it.

How it works

One sentence to start

01

Say it

Talk or type. “I want the house.” “I want to stop shrinking in meetings.” You don't have to word it well — Evella asks the next question if it needs one.

02

Evella writes it

Your sentence becomes a three-minute narrated meditation — set where you live, with the people you named in it, spoken as if it's already the case.

03

Play it tomorrow morning

And the morning after. Every session teaches it a little more about you, so what comes back gets more exact — and more like your own voice.
